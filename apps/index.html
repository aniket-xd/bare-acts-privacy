Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how these Android apps, published by Akinchan Aggarwal, handle information: Court Fee Calculator (com.courtfee.calculator), Arbitration Fee Calculator (com.arbitrationfee.calculator) and Legal Dictionary (com.legalshabdavali.app).

Information the apps store

You don't need an account. Amounts you type are used on your device only. Your chosen state stays on your device. Quiz scores are not saved. We don't receive anything you enter, and uninstalling an app deletes what it stored.

Advertising

The apps show banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process device and usage information, such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and interactions with ads. This information is sent to Google, encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

Information we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and we don't sell personal information.

Children

The apps are intended for adults and aren't directed at children under 13.

Not government apps

These apps are independent and aren't affiliated with or endorsed by any court, government body or bar association. They are for reference only and aren't legal advice.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
