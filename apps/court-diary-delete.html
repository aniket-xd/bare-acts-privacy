Delete your Court Diary account

Court Diary (com.courtdiary.india) is published by Akinchan Aggarwal.

In the app

  1. Open Court Diary and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

The web version (court-diary.pages.dev) has the same option under Settings > Account.

By email

If you can't use the app, email aniketaggarwal0@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Court Diary account". We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, email address).
  • Your membership of your team.
  • If you are the only member of your team, the whole team diary: cases, hearing dates, ticks, to-do tasks, notes, lists and tags.

What is kept

  • If other members remain in your team, the cases and tasks in the shared diary stay with them, because they belong to the team. Ownership passes to another member.
  • Nothing else is kept.
